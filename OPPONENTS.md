## Don't want to be played?

Anyone can type any GitHub username into Stard, so your public stars can become a team even if you never played. Either of these stops it:

- **Add a file called `no-play`** (any content) to a public repo called `stard` on your account. Within an hour, nobody else can play against your stars: you are never an opponent, your team links stop opening, and your stars are read only for runs you play yourself.
- **Ask me** in an issue (https://github.com/armanckeser/github-stars-organizer/issues/new?title=Don%27t%20play%20me) or a message on GitHub. Within a few days your username goes on a private block list: nobody can play against your stars or play as you, and old team links with your name stop opening.

## Your character

Each account here has a character, the figure behind its cards, drawn by hand from its public GitHub avatar and kept in [`characters/`](characters/). Only plain parts are used: hair, glasses, top and colours.

- **Change it:** add a file called `character.md` to a public repo called `stard` on your account ([the keys](characters/README.md#the-keys)). Your file always wins over the one here.
- **Remove it:** open an issue (https://github.com/armanckeser/github-stars-organizer/issues/new?title=Remove%20my%20character) or message me on GitHub, and the file is deleted.

An account that opted out above never shows the character drawn here, anywhere in Stard.
